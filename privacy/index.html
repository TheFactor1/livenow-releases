Live Now privacy policy

Live Now is made by Factor. Effective October 9, 2026.

Live Now is a sports app that shows live games and scores and plays streams from a TV service you already subscribe to. It does not include, sell or recommend any streaming service.

What we collect

Nothing. Live Now has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own that receive your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your provider login (server address, username and password). It is stored encrypted with a key held in Android's Keystore, used only to connect to your provider, and left out of backups and device transfers.
  • Your teams, followed games and settings. Stored on the phone only.
  • Stream sync. Text recognition reads a broadcast's scoreboard on your phone, through Google Play services. Images are not sent anywhere by Live Now.

Who the app talks to

  • Your TV service, using the login you entered, to list channels and play streams.
  • Public sports data services (such as MLB, NHL and ESPN), for schedules, scores, team lists and logos. These requests carry no personal information. Like any web request, the service sees your IP address.
  • YouTube, for highlight videos, through the YouTube embedded player. By watching highlights you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to that player.
  • Demo mode plays freely licensed test videos from public video test servers. Like any web request, those servers see your IP address.

Notifications

If you turn on game alerts, the app schedules them on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

Live Now is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.
